About MindGuard

MindGuard is a predictive mental health risk classification system powered by machine learning. It analyses key behavioural and demographic indicators to estimate an individual's risk of experiencing mental health challenges.

How It Works

1

User completes an 8-question behavioural assessment.

2

The AI model evaluates risk indicators across all responses.

3

A risk score and classification (Low / Moderate / High) is generated.

4

Personalised recommendations are provided based on the result.

Family History Mental Health History Habit Changes Mood Swings Days Indoors Work Disinterest Care Access Occupation
